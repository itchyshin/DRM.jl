# After-Task Report: the #574 resolvable-scale guard had zero margin — the #461 runaway was still reachable

- **Date:** 2026-09-05
- **Issue:** #461 / #574 follow-up. Two `test/test_bootstrap_marginal.jl` assertions
  failing on `origin/main`; branch `claude/parity-drmjl-main-failures-drmjl` off
  DRM.jl `origin/main` `345892520`
- **Perspectives:** Gauss (engine), Rose (after-task)

## 1. Goal

Close two test failures sitting on DRM.jl `main`, named by the drmTMB parity leaf
behind PR #1226 (recorded in drmTMB
`docs/dev-log/after-task/2026-09-05-a8c-response-mask.md` §10 as "pre-existing
DRM.jl failures, unrelated and NOT fixed here"): in
`test/test_bootstrap_marginal.jl`, testset "#461 a degenerate optimum is not
reported as converged -- converges under the #574 guard", `res.failed == 0`
evaluating `1 == 0` and `res.used == 60` evaluating `59 == 60`. Either fix the
engine or, if the test's expectation had gone wrong, fix the test and say why.

## 2. Implemented

One constant in `src/location_only.jl`, from `0.5 * log(eps)` to
`0.25 * log(eps)`, plus the derivation in the comment above it; one new
path-independent testset in `test/test_lss_phylo.jl`; a NEWS entry.

The failures are real and the engine was wrong. `_loconly_resolvable_scales`
refuses the sparse Woodbury objective when `sigma^2 / sigma_phy^2 < eps`. The
relative error of that subtraction grows as `eps * (sigma_phy^2 / sigma^2)`, so
`eps` is exactly the ratio at which no digits of the result survive: #574 put the
bar *on* the cliff, with zero margin, and the objective is still wrong just above
it. The bar is now `sqrt(eps)` on the variance ratio — keep at least half the
mantissa, the standard rule for a cancellation-limited difference.

## 3a. Decisions and rejected alternatives

- **Rejected: loosen the assertion.** `res.failed == 0` states a true intention
  (no replicate lands on a spurious `+Inf` optimum). It was failing because the
  intention was not yet met, not because it had gone stale.
- **Rejected: tune the constant until the fixture passes.** The bar is derived
  from the cancellation error of the Woodbury subtraction and measured against a
  dense oracle (§5); it leaves ~8 nats of headroom over the observed break, not
  the 0.94 nats #574 left.
- **Rejected: an absolute floor on `sigma`.** The measurement shows the objective
  is accurate at `sigma_phy = 2e-12` provided the RATIO holds, so the ratio is the
  right axis and an absolute floor would refuse representable points.
- **Kept: `is_converged` / `_nondegenerate_fit` in `src/summary.jl`.** It is the
  backstop that caught this and it is untouched.

## 6. Tests of the tests

The existing `#461` testset is **not** a reliable pin and this is the finding
worth keeping. It depends on whether the optimiser happens to walk into the
unresolvable sliver, which is decided by rounding: on the identical source,
seeds and `B = 60`, it is **green on x86_64 Linux CI** (`Julia 1.10 - shard 2/4`
of run 34003739922, the run for the merge that became `345892520`: "#461 a
degenerate optimum ... | 6 6 1.5s") and **red on aarch64 macOS**. It was already
red locally at `aaecac45a`, the very commit that flipped it to `res.used == 60`
and whose message claims GREEN — that commit's own receipt records "Julia 1.10 is
not installed locally".

The new testset in `test/test_lss_phylo.jl` evaluates the objective directly and
is path-independent. It sweeps `log(sigma) - log(sigma_phy)` PAST #574's old bar
and asserts the property — every pair the guard ADMITS must match the dense
oracle to 1e-06 relative — so refused points are skipped and only re-loosening
the bar can fail it. It also asserts `admitted >= 20`, because an empty sweep is
not a pass.

## 10. Known residuals

- Only the univariate Gaussian phylogenetic location-only ML route. REML,
  `sd_phylo ~ x`, bivariate, non-Gaussian and the R bridge are untouched and
  unmeasured here.
- The bar is a representation limit for THIS Woodbury form. Nothing here says
  the other structured objectives (`gaussian_structured.jl`,
  `gaussian_sparse_lss.jl`) have adequate margin; they were not audited.
- Measured on one platform pair (aarch64 macOS vs x86_64 Linux CI) and one Julia

  diagnosed, was not re-measured.
- The second failure the drmTMB a8c leaf reported alongside these two —
  `test_lss_missing_response.jl`, `Package StableRNGs not found` — was already
  fixed on `main` by `764d4f453` (StableRNGs added to `test/Project.toml`);
  verified green here at 57/57, not fixed by this branch.

## 11. Team learning

A guard placed exactly at the limit it names has no margin, and a numerical bar
with no margin is decided by rounding — which makes it a platform coin flip and
makes any test that depends on the optimiser's path an unreliable pin. Derive
the bar from how much precision the operation destroys, then step back from it;
and pin a numerical boundary by evaluating the objective, not by watching where
an optimiser lands.
